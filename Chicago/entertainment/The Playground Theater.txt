Description: The Playground Theater is a continuously operating non-profit theater in Chicago dedicated to Modern Theatrical Improvisation, a form of theater invented in the city. The Playground was founded in 1997 by a collective of Chicago improvisors. Originally located on Halsted Street, the theater now produces shows at different theatrical venues across Chicago. The Playground Theater was created to provide a Chicago venue where improvisors could have complete artistic control over the work they produced.
