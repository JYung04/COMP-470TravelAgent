Description: Significantly more polished than your typical BBQ joint, Chicago Q in downtown’s Gold Coast boasts rich leather couches and gleaming dark wood tables, as well as a critically acclaimed pit master. Billed as the new urban BBQ experience, the pits are manned by none other than celebrity chef Art Smith, who was a personal chef for Oprah Winfrey. Expect artisanal housemade rubs, the highest quality meats, and service that exudes Southern hospitality.
