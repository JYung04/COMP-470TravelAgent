Description: Batter and Berries Known as one of the city’s best brunches, Batter & Berries is a colorful BYOB brunch spot in the Lincoln Park neighborhood. And don’t miss out on their hearty deconstructed omelets and house specialties like the Cluck-N-Gaufre — a stuffed sweet potato waffle served with buttermilk herb fried chicken.
