Description: Inspired by traditional Mexican street food like suadero, esquites and squash blossom quesadillas, Hunger Street Tacos serves fresh, affordable tacos and quesadillas with rich flavor. All of their tacos are priced under $5, with options ranging from classic carnitas to brisket and chorizo breakfast tacos with refried beans, avocado salsa, lime and eggs. They have several veggie-friendly fillings as well, like sauteed mushrooms, black beans, squash blossom salsa and fire roasted poblano peppers.
