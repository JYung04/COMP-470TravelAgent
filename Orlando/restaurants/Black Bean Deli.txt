Description: Longtime Orlando staple Black Bean Deli serves up traditional Cuban fare in Winter Park and Mills 50. Score classic sandwiches like Media Noche ($10) or Pan con Lechon ($11), or create a shareable feast with a medley of Cuban bowls ($15), plates ($18) and sides like empanadas, maduros and ham croquetas ($1 – $6). They also have a great $10 Cuban sandwich special, which includes a 6-inch sandwich and a cup of black beans and rice daily through 3 p.m.
