Description: Best of Boston winner, 2001 (Best Seafood, Affordable) , 2002 (Best Seafood, Affordable) , and 2005 (Best Seafood, Affordable) You can’t miss that bright red- and yellow-striped big top tent on the waterfront. This Seaport fixture beckons with its boisterous crowds gathered for classic crab-shack eats: platters piled high with crustaceans and drawn butter dip; fried-seafood standards—like Ipswich clams or Rhode Island calamari; and plenty of cold beer to wash it all down. The space is strung with lobster traps and strands of lights; it ain’t fancy, but it’s a lot of fun. 88 Sleeper St., Seaport District, Boston, 617-426-2722, barkingcrab.com .
