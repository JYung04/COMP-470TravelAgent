Description: The Frattaroli family opened Lucia nearly 50 years ago, and the wide-ranging menu continues to honor their roots in Italy’s Abruzzo region. An icon on the menu helpfully marks traditional dishes from the area, such as chitarra al tartufo, a pasta dish with mushrooms, sausage, and white truffle cream; guazzetto di mare, a seafood medley in tomato sauce over pappardelle; and carrozza, a must-order app of breaded, fried mozzarella stuffed with prosciutto and topped with marinara. And, perhaps a rarity for such a flour-focused restaurant: Lucia offers plenty of gluten-free options. 415 Hanover St., North End, Boston, 617-367-2353, luciab.st .
