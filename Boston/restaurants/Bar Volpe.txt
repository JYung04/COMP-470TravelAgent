Description: Yes, celebrated chef Karen Akunowicz serves pristine Best of Boston-worthy pasta at her southern Italian spot. Even better: Make a reservation and mention a gluten allergy, and the servers will have a separate gluten-free menu already waiting at the table. 170 W.
