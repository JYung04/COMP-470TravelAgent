Description: Since its founding in 1936, the ICA has shared the pleasures of reflection, inspiration, imagination, and provocation that contemporary art offers with its audiences. A museum at the intersection of contemporary art and civic life, the ICA has advanced a bold vision for amplifying the artist’s voice and expanding the museum’s role as educator, incubator, and convener. There is always something new and exciting to see at the ICA. Situated in Boston’s vibrant Seaport neighborhood, the ICA hosts some of today’s most exciting artists, musicians, DJs, dancers, and performers. The museum's variety of exhibitions, performances, and programs provides access to the breadth of contemporary art and artists, inviting audiences of all ages and backgrounds to participate in the excitement of new art and ideas. Don't forget to stop by the award-winning ICA Store, featuring a thoughtfully curated selection of gifts.
