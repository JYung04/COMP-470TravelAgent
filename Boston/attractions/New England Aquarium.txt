Name: New England Aquarium
City: Boston
Category: attractions
Cuisine: Not listed
Address: 1 Central Wharf, Boston MA 02110
Description: Experience the wonder of 70 exhibits featuring aquatic animals from around the world. Get your hands wet at The Trust Family Foundation Shark and Ray Touch Tank. Watch Northern fur seals at the New Balance Foundation Marine Mammal Center. Finally, experience an unforgettable film on New England’s largest screen. The New England Aquarium is located on Central Wharf, just steps from historic Faneuil Hall, the Rose Kennedy Greenway, and right on the bustling waterfront. Spend your day at the Aquarium. Visit our exhibits, watch a nature film, and during the months of April-October take a Whale Watch trip which departs right from the Aquarium. Lunch packages are available at the Aquarium cafe and on board the Whale Watch or dine at restaurants in Faneuil Hall. This Business is Group Friendly! SUMMER HOURS June – August Monday – Thursday: 9:00 a.m..
