Description: Franklin Park Zoo, a 72-acre site nestled within Boston's historic Franklin Park, is operated and managed by Zoo New England. Conservation is the cornerstone of Zoo New England's mission, and we are committed to preserving and protecting the incredible biodiversity of this planet both locally and globally. At Franklin Park Zoo, guests will discover more than 50 species of animals, including mammals, reptiles, birds, and amphibians. At Nature's Neighborhoods, the award-winning Children's Zoo, get up close with the prairie dogs and climb atop the Eagle's Nest for a bird's eye view of the Zoo. Be sure to visit the African lion in the Kalahari Kingdom, the Masai giraffe on the Giraffe Savannah, and the many remarkable species that call the Zoo home.
