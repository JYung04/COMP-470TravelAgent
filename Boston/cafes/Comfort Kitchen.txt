Description: This Best of Boston Dorchester spot has snagged accolades and recognition from near and far since its 2023 opening in Upham’s Corner . It might as well take home another trophy solely for how detailed the menu is, labeling menu items as gluten-free so you won’t feel like you’re pestering the servers. Most of the menu is gluten-free or can be made that way with a few quick adjustments. Blue-ribbon bites include the beef kafta with yogurt tahini and roasted duck with bold jerk spices that’s served with rice and peas, plus a zippy pickled relish condiment called pikliz. End things on a sweet note with a slice of Basque cheesecake. 611 Columbia Rd., Dorchester, Boston, 617-329-6910, comfortkitchenbos.com .
