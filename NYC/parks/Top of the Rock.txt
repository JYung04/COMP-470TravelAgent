Description: The Rockefeller Center Observation Deck , atop the historic 30 Rock building, offers spectacular views of Midtown landmarks, including the Empire State Building and Central Park. Opened in 1933, the attraction was inspired by the grand ocean liners of the early 20th century, and its fixtures are appropriately luxurious.
