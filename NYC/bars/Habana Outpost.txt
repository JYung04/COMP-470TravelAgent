Description: When the weather’s nice, enjoy the colorful decorations and shaded patio at the seasonal Habana Outpost . The spot serves up what some say is the best Cuban sandwich in New York, great elote (Mexican-style grilled corn) and on-point tacos; it also hosts outdoor movie nights. Bonus: across the street, on the side of the building that holds Habana’s takeout spot, there’s an awesome mural of Notorious B.I.G. (who was born nearby). Courtesy, Greenlight Bookstore
