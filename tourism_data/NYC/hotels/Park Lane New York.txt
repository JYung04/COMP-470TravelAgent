Description: The Park Lane Hotel is a luxury hotel at 36 Central Park South, between Fifth and Sixth Avenues in Midtown Manhattan, New York City. Constructed in 1971, the hotel was designed by Emery Roth & Sons for real estate developer Harry Helmsley. The hotel was owned by Helmsley-Spear, the real estate development company run by Harry and wife Leona Helmsley, until it was sold to Steve Witkoff's Witkoff Group in 2013. Witkoff proposed a supertall skyscraper for the site but later canceled these plans. Following a fraud investigation involving a co-owner, Jho Low, a stake in the hotel was sold to the Mubadala Investment Company in 2019. The Qatar Investment Authority (QIA) bought the hotel in 2023.
