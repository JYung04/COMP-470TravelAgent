Description: 234 W. 44th St. (between Broadway and Eighth Ave.) This landmark restaurant opened in 1921 and quickly became a legendary Broadway watering hole. Once the nightspot of choice for the stars of the Great White Way, Sardi's still attracts occasional live celebrity clients to accompany the numerous caricature portraits that line its walls. The restaurant covers four full floors, but those looking for stronger drinks and star sightings should head downstairs to the bar.
