Description: Hamilton Fish Park is a public park on the Lower East Side of Manhattan in New York City. Named after former New York governor Hamilton Fish, the park was built on two blocks bounded by Houston, Pitt, Sheriff, and Stanton Streets. It contains a playground, basketball courts, and an outdoor swimming complex with general swimming and wading pools. Hamilton Fish Park also includes a Beaux-Arts recreation center designed by Carrère and Hastings. It is maintained by the New York City Department of Parks and Recreation.
