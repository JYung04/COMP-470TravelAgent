Description: Cool off with a full-day of indoor fun on International Drive courtesy of the 800,000-square-foot Dezerland Park . It’s home to affordably priced attractions for all ages, including Karting Orlando, Jump Start Trampoline Park, Bowling Orlando, The Pinball Palace, Arcade Orlando and The Brass Axe. Dezerland Park’s centerpiece is the 250,000-square-foot Orlando Auto Museum. You can also grab a meal at the Bond Lounge & Exhibit, a sit-down restaurant with a massive collection of Bond memorabilia.
