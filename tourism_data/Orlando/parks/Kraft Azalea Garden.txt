Description: Billed as Winter Park’s secret garden, Kraft Azalea Gardens is a serene, 5-acre waterfront park with towering cypress trees, scenic lake views and of course, brilliant azaleas. Perfect for painting, picnicking or lounging in the shade, it’s also home to a beautiful exedra, a great egret rookery and the largest surviving banyan tree north of Miami. The garden is named for Winter Park residents George and Maud Kraft, who helped to establish the park in 1938.
