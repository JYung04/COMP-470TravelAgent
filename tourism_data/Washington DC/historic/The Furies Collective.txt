Description: The Furies Collective was a short-lived commune of twelve young lesbian separatists in Washington, D.C., U.S. in 1971 and 1972. They viewed lesbianism as more political than sexual, and declared heterosexual women to be an obstacle to the world revolution they sought. Their theories are still acknowledged among feminist groups.
