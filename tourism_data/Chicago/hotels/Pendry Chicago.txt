Description: The Carbide & Carbon Building is an Art Deco high-rise built in 1929, located on Michigan Avenue in Chicago, Illinois. The 37-story, 503 ft (153 m) landmark is clad in black granite, green and gold terra cotta, with gold leaf and bronze trim. It was converted into a hotel in 2004, operating under license from Hard Rock International until 2017, rebranding as the St. Jane Hotel and then the Pendry Chicago Hotel.
