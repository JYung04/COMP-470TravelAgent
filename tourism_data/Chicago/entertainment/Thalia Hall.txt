Description: Thalia Hall is a landmark concert venue in Chicago’s Pilsen neighborhood . Designed after the Prague opera house, Thalia Hall first opened in 1892 as an arts and entertainment space for the community. Today, the concert hall hosts shows by well-known musical acts, up-and-coming local names, touring comedians, and more. The venue also houses a retro cocktail lounge, a piano bar, and a modern Mexican seafood restaurant.
