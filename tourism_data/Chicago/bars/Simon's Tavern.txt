Description: This Andersonville hole-in-the-wall has been a favorite local hangout since it opened in 1934. Back then, Simon’s was a grocery store with a subterranean speakeasy for the area’s early immigrants. Today, the neighborhood’s Swedish roots are still on display at Simon’s — you’ll find Viking paraphernalia on the walls and glögg (a traditional mulled wine) behind the bar. Unlike some speakeasies, it’s easy enough to find.
