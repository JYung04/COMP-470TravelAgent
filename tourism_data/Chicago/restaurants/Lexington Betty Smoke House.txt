Description: Lexington Betty Smokehouse Adding a soulful touch to Chicago-style grilled fare, Lexington Betty Smokehouse in Roseland is a Black woman-owned eatery that’s redefining Midwestern barbecue. Chef Dominique Leach transitioned from classical cooking to helm her own barbecue hub in her hometown. As pitmaster, she smokes a great range of meat, from chicken wings to pork shoulder, to pair with addictive side dishes like collard greens, candied yams, and corn muffins.
