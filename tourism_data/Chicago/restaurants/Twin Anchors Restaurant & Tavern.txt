Description: One power player on the city’s barbecue scene is an oldie, but a goodie. The Old Town institution Twin Anchors Restaurant & Tavern hasn’t changed much since it was established in 1932, and the crowds (which at one time included Frank Sinatra) appreciate it that way. Don’t miss their tender, meaty baby back ribs, which have been on the menu for over 86 years.
