Description: After completing a multi-million dollar transformation, The Langham, Boston begins a new era with stylistic features, refined elegance and unique experiences that have been redefined into this new American classic. Prominently located on the beautiful Norman B. Leventhal Park at Post Office Square in the very heart of Boston, The Langham, Boston is just steps away from Faneuil Hall Marketplace, and a short walk to the North End, Theatre District, Waterfront and the city’s renowned historic attractions. Housed in the former Federal Reserve Bank of Boston, a national historic landmark, The Langham’s Renaissance-Revival granite and limestone structure was designed by R. Clipton Sturgis and modeled after the Palazzo della Cancellaria in Rome. Elegant and distinctly European, The Langham gracefully tempers history with charm and urban sophistication and provides your guests with an exceptional array of amenities and services to fulfill.
