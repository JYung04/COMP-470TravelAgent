Description: The USS Constitution Museum serves as the memory and educational voice of USS Constitution, by collecting, preserving and interpreting the stories of "Old Ironsides." Open 7 days a week, the Museum is located directly across from the USS Constitution and invites families and guests to swing in a hammock, furl a sail or enlist onboard the USS Constitution as a sailor in 1812! Our hands-on, minds-on, award-winning exhibitions create positive and memorable experiences for all. Admission is by donation. This Museum is Group Friendly! We welcome individuals, families and group tours. Please call ahead to arrange a group visit. Set sail on a voyage of discovery by visiting the USS Constitution Museum! Located in the Charlestown Navy Yard, the Museum provides a hands-on, minds-on environment where groups can have fun and learn as they explore Constitution ’s history together. Self-guided.
