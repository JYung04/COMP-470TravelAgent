Description: Prudential Center is a destination designed for discovery and experience. Located in the heart of Boston’s historic Back Bay, Prudential Center features over 70 shops, eateries and attractions including major brands such as Saks Fifth Avenue, Aritzia, Canada Goose, Eataly Boston, Sephora, and DICK'S House of Sport. Start your Boston experience 750ft up at View Boston, Prudential Center’s observatory featuring 360° breathtaking indoor and outdoor views and interactive experiences. Once you have viewed the city from above, jump on a Duck Tour to experience the city by land and water. After groups have had a chance to take in the sights, shop and dine at the Pru taking advantage of the complimentary discount shopper card and premiere events! Relax in Prudential Center’s South Garden, a 1.3 acre open-air park located directly inside the Center, providing a unique urban oasis complete.
