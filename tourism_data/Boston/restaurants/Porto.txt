Description: Boston’s venerable chef Jody Adams brings Back Bay her expectedly marvelous musings on contemporary Mediterranean seafood. Think octopus a la plancha with smoked tomato puree; whole roasted branzino that gets a lift from garlic, oregano, and orange; and seared scallops with sunchokes, pomegranate, and warm gooseberry vinaigrette. Adams has been inducted to the James Beard Foundation’s “Who’s Who” list of legends. We can see, and taste, why. 1 Ring Rd., Back Bay, Boston, 617-536-1234, porto-boston.com .
