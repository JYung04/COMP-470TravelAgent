Description: Known as a true neighborhood bar, Harvard Gardens is located across the street from Mass General Hospital and attracts a diverse crowd of area workers and Beacon Hill residents. At night the dining room glows with candlelight while the handsome bar hosts a great selection of beers, wines, whiskeys and inventive drinks. Holding one of the first liquor licenses issued after prohibition, Harvard Gardens enjoys an old time speak-easy vibe, which allows for a friendly, unpretentious comfort to its guests.
